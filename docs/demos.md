## Format

The copies use H.264 video, a width of 1280 pixels, 20 frames per second, AAC audio and fast-start MP4 metadata. They preserve the full recording timeline and audio tracks, with small end-of-stream timing differences below 0.03 seconds from frame-rate conversion. They were not shortened into excerpts.
